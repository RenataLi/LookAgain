# Wider-source feasibility for a prospective detail-availability experiment

Checked 2026-09-26. Primary sources only. Initial web inspection was followed by a census of the author's small metadata and a source-only geometry check of one PDF in the author's 5 MB sample archive. No full corpus archive, weights, or model outcomes were inspected. This is a feasibility assessment, not a locked cohort or a claim that 600 eligible documents exist. This is a historical planning note; the [study index](../reports/README.md) describes the subsequent completed DUDE experiment.

## DUDE: a credible public route, with eligibility and geometry still to establish

**Access and provenance.** The [authors' DUDE repository](https://github.com/duchallenge-team/dude) links both the registration-based RRC competition portal and the public author-maintained Hugging Face loader. The latter is an explicitly recommended distribution route, not an unofficial mirror. The repository describes original PDFs and three provided OCR systems: Amazon Textract, Azure, and Tesseract. Tesseract inputs were PDF pages converted to TIFF at 200 DPI. RRC registration is therefore not the only author-published route.

The [author HF repository](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jordyvl/DUDE_loader/tree/b3662175d3b2482d711f18559b7acc2a5bccc600) currently resolves to commit `b3662175d3b2482d711f18559b7acc2a5bccc600`. Its [data directory](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jordyvl/DUDE_loader/tree/b3662175d3b2482d711f18559b7acc2a5bccc600/data) lists `DUDE_train-val-test_binaries.tar.gz` at approximately **21.2 GB**; the full repository is approximately 21.4 GB. It also contains a 121 MB sample PDF archive, which must not be assumed to contain enough independent eligible training documents. The normal loader downloads and extracts the entire large archive. No efficient author-published per-document PDF endpoint was verified here. Download plus extraction requires more disk space than the archive size alone.

**Exact small annotation route.** The [author's Zenodo release](https://zenodo.org/records/7763635), DOI `10.5281/zenodo.7763635`, provides [2023-03-23_DUDE_gt_test_PUBLIC.json](https://zenodo.org/records/7763635/files/2023-03-23_DUDE_gt_test_PUBLIC.json?download=1), approximately **14.1 MB**, published MD5 `bf252b2dc57d501b2d3110a4e7e6e9c5`. Despite its filename, this is the loader's combined annotation source: training and validation annotations plus blind test entries. Restrict a census to `data_split == "train"` before eligibility. The release advertises 23,728 training rows and 6,315 validation rows; its displayed release/version and aggregate counts differ across revisions. Compute SHA256 and actual counts from the retrieved bytes rather than treating those descriptions as the final manifest.

**Documents and task.** The [ICCV paper, sections 3.2–3.5 and Tables 1–2](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.08455v3) reports **3,010 train documents**, 749 validation documents, and 1,215 test documents. These sum to 4,974; Table 1 separately reports 5,019 collected documents. It mixes born-digital files and scans across industries, with extractive, abstractive, list, and unanswerable questions. Extractive question annotation included evidence bounding boxes. Thus it has a substantially larger starting pool, but the paper's counts do not establish the number surviving our one-document/one-question, single-page, short-span, small-ROI rules. The paper's official answer metric is ANLS; this is not TAT-DQA official EM.

**Actual downloaded release differs from the paper.** JSON SHA256 `4f883956ba02cb676022ec24878cdc415e759438e945c2d479bdae488873e2e8`, 14,092,824 bytes, declares version 1.0.8 and contains 41,456 rows: 23,736 train, 6,318 validation, 11,402 test. Training has **2,974 distinct docIds**, 10,679 extractive questions; requiring one nonempty answer of at most 160 characters and 25 whitespace-delimited words gives 10,556 questions on **2,899 docIds**. These independently recomputed counts are metadata candidates, not verified independent original sources or geometry-eligible examples.

**Annotation and pixel contract.** The [pinned loader source](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jordyvl/DUDE_loader/blob/bfa5bcd170ed554092161beba3ce5e8a335b6931/DUDE_loader.py) exposes `docId`, `questionId`, `question`, `answers`, `answers_variants`, `answer_type`, and `answers_page_bounding_boxes` with `left`, `top`, `width`, `height`, `page`. Original PDF paths are `PDF/<split>/<docId>.pdf`. Its explicit PDF rendering helper uses 200 DPI. A PDF has no universal native pixel resolution; scanning resolution and render resolution must be distinguished. Coordinate units and answer-page indexing were **not verified** from this schema. The loader coerces box fields to integers and flattens the first nested box list; retain raw JSON until those assumptions are checked. It also calls built-in `open()` on the annotation URL, so the historical loader should not be assumed executable unchanged.

The [author ImDB conversion script](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jordyvl/DUDE_loader/blob/b3662175d3b2482d711f18559b7acc2a5bccc600/DUDE_imdb_loader.py) converts Amazon OCR pages from one-based to zero-based indices, but that does **not** establish the indexing of human answer boxes. Its generated `answer_page` is `None`; do not substitute this processed representation for raw localization annotations. Prefer a fresh, pinned PDF page-rendering and coordinate-validation adapter.

**Terms.** The HF dataset card and loader label the dataset **CC BY 4.0**. The [author's per-document license/source lineage CSV](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jordyvl/DUDE_loader/blob/b3662175d3b2482d711f18559b7acc2a5bccc600/licenses_metadata_datalineage.csv), approximately 635 KB, should accompany the census and attribution mapping; local parsing found 4,615 rows. The license label is blank in 4,463 rows, but licenseurl is blank in 3,507; some URLs explicitly indicate noncommercial/share-alike/no-derivative terms. All master_hash values are blank. Preserve both license columns; missing entries are incomplete metadata, not an inferred prohibition or verified permission. The GitHub benchmark repository is GPL-3.0, the HF loader header Apache-2.0, and the [standalone author evaluator](https://github.com/Jordy-VL/DUDEeval) MIT. These are different artifacts, not interchangeable blanket terms for source PDFs.

## One finance alternative: FinanceBench does not solve the sample-size constraint

The [creator's public repository](https://github.com/patronus-ai/financebench) supplies **150 public QA examples**, financial PDFs, evidence text, and explicitly zero-indexed evidence pages. Its documented schema has no evidence bounding-box field. The larger 10,231-question collection is not the public download; the authors request contact for it. The [creator's HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/PatronusAI/financebench) labels the 150 rows as a `train` split and declares **CC BY-NC 4.0**. That split label does not turn the original released evaluation sample into a new research training corpus. Answers include reasoning and units rather than only copyable spans. It is a legitimate finance document source, but at most 150 public questions cannot yield more than 600 independent eligible source documents, and ROI construction would add a new annotation/alignment stage.

## Proposed decision boundary, before outcomes

The completed local metadata census left 10,432 questions on 2,897 docIds with structurally valid rectangles on one annotated answer page. Of those docIds, 2,489 have a matching lineage row and 408 do not. Two duplicate question-ID groups in the raw release are identical abstractive records and do not enter the extractive pool. These counts do not establish eligible independent PDFs. The source-only specimen `0017b64bd017f06db47e56a6a113e22e` is reserved for engineering and must be excluded from a future main cohort.

Count original documents, not questions, and expose each exclusion before deterministic selection. Require a single nonempty extractive answer, supported page/ROI, no unresolved alternative answer locations, and source-only checks for legibility, exact answer support, PDF geometry and a bounded ROI. Do not relax these requirements to reach a target N after viewing model accuracy. Source URLs/byte hashes should also identify duplicate or reused original files across datasets.

The census supported proceeding to archive-level geometry and source review. The resulting [DUDE study](dude_replication_protocol.md) used a **selected training subset for a cross-dataset extension** of the matched-detail comparison. Its reference variants, metrics and semantic-review rules were fixed separately from the financial-report study; TAT-DQA scoring was not transferred to DUDE. Public benchmark exposure in VLM pretraining is unknown. Privileged evidence ROIs remain a positive control for information availability, not an evaluated deployable localizer.
