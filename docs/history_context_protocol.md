## Exploratory decision rule

Evaluate each history separately, including the actual control, with the fixed practical threshold 0.02 (2 pp) applied to its absolute D_h, not only to an interaction. An interface becomes a candidate for **validation on new, untouched original reports** only when:

1. D_h is at least 0.02;
2. its paired 95% bootstrap lower bound is strictly above zero; and
3. its native mean conservative EM is at least the fresh direct mean.

Report every component and all histories, not only a winner. The primary interaction remains the primary scientific contrast whether or not a candidate gate passes. Multiple candidate checks and secondary metrics are exploratory and unadjusted. Inspect measured cost, harms and highres performance before deciding the scope of subsequent validation.

Passing this rule motivates a separate learnability study and untouched evaluation. A failed rule applies to this panel and tested interfaces; it does not establish general equivalence. Near-zero effects are reported with the empirical-variance limitation above. Method novelty is outside the scope of this diagnostic.
